About Us

Island Shipping Corporation has been serving the Cebu region for years, providing reliable and safe ferry services connecting various islands and ports. We pride ourselves on our commitment to safety, punctuality, and customer service.

Operating from multiple branches including Tangil, Hagnaya, and Port Bulado, we ensure seamless connectivity for both passengers and cargo throughout the central Visayas region. Our modern fleet and experienced crew guarantee your journey is both comfortable and secure.
